Listed buildings to review

Every house beside the National Heritage List entries within 40m of it, so the whole estate can be settled here rather than one house at a time on the map. Each entry links to its record on the Historic England site.

The list records a house, its boundary walls and its gate piers as separate entries, so the nearest one is often not the house — and a house can hold several. This is the one records the house's own entry and marks it as listed; Also this house adds the others, as many as belong to it. Either takes that entry out of every other house's suggestions. Not this house dismisses it here only.
