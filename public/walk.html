Nottingham Park Houses

The Park Walk

Four hundred houses, eight thousand people, and a hundred and eighty years of them. Walk the estate and it tells you what happened where you are standing — and asks you the handful of things it cannot work out from paper.

How long have you got?

What are you in the mood for?

On an evening in each of these years a man walked The Park with a book, door by door, doubling back where the houses made him. Pick his round and you walk it in his order, with each household as he met it.

Your phone's location is used to put you on the map and to know when you are near a stop. It stays on your phone — nothing is sent anywhere unless you answer a question.
