Who is reading it

Counted here, by us, and nobody else. No cookie is set and no address is kept — a reader is a scrambled code made from their connection and browser with a number that changes at midnight, so two visits on one day can be told apart and nothing of it lasts into the next. That is why the site asks nobody's permission to count.

The last couple of days

Robots — search engines indexing the record, uptime checks — are left out by default; the Robots button puts them back, and your own visits are never counted while you are signed in as admin. Counting starts the day this goes live, so the first few days will look thin. Nothing older than four months is kept.
