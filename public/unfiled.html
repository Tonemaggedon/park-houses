Unfiled census records

Records filed against no property, gathered by the address they carry rather than listed one by one. Most of the backlog is a handful of addresses repeated many times, so the whole of one can be settled in a single click.

Where a house name appears in the text and the group holds one household, the match is offered outlined in green. A street alone is only a street. And a group holding several households is flagged as such: a house name in the text evidences one of them, not all of them.

Each household can be filed on its own. Every block below has its own box and button, so a street holding six different houses is six decisions rather than one wrong one. "File all" is only right when the group really is one household.

Set aside — the census cannot place these

The return gave no house name and no number, only the street, so no amount of census review will settle them. They are still in the record and still searchable; they are only out of the queue, waiting on a different source — a directory, a rate book, a deed, or somebody's memory.

Nothing here is applied for you. Filing a group also links each person to the property, exactly as filing one record does.
