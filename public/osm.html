Positions against OpenStreetMap

A good many of this record's positions were reasoned rather than surveyed — placed from the enumerator's round, or from a house name on a map that is a diagram and not a survey. OpenStreetMap holds a real outline and often a real address for every building in The Park. Where the two disagree by more than a few metres, one of them is wrong.

OpenStreetMap is not gospel either. Its numbering can be wrong, and a house split into flats may carry a number that belongs to its neighbour. This flags what is worth looking at, not what to change.
