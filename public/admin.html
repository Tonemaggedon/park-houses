Admin tools

Jobs that change many records at once. Where a tool has a Preview, it shows exactly what would happen and changes nothing — the other button does the work. Each result appears under the tool you ran.

These tools are for the site admin. Sign in as admin on the map page, then come back here.

Bring files in

Files prepared in data/ and pushed to the site. Both are safe to run twice — neither makes a duplicate.

Import people

Adds the people, census records, occupations and relationships in the prepared people_*.json files, and links them to their houses. Matches on person number or name, and only fills blanks on anyone already in the record.

Import bibliographies

Loads each prepared works list onto the person who wrote them.

Move records

For a few people at a time, Split a House's Census and Unfiled records are quicker. This is for a whole house's worth.

Move a household to another property

Moves every census record from one property to another, and the resident links with them. Leave to blank to unfile them instead, back to the unfiled queue. Give person numbers to move one household out of a house holding several.

Fill gaps from the census

The census records often know more than the person records do. These copy it across — never overwriting anything already filled in.

Date occupations from the census

Gives an undated occupation the years of the person's own census records that name it — "Nurse" dated from an 1891 return saying "Nurse (domestic)".

Copy census occupations onto people

So they show on the People page, the group chips and the occupation filter.

Copy birth places onto people

Fills a blank birth place from the census. Anyone whose census records disagree with each other is left alone and listed for you to settle.

Clean up after imports

Pasted spreadsheets sometimes slip a column or bring their headings with them. These find the damage.

Find rows that are not people

Lists records whose name is a column heading, or blank. Deletes nothing — each links to its record, where the delete button is.

Clear occupations that are not occupations

A sex, a relationship ("Servant", "wife") or a dash left in the occupation field, skewing the counts. Preview names every one before anything is cleared.

Recover the slipped source column

Where "Married, Female" landed in the source field: the sex fills a blank on the person, the marital status moves to its own column, and only then is the source cleared.

Remove duplicate resident links

The same person linked to the same house twice. Keeps one of each.

Remove census rows a second import left behind

Somebody with two records for one census year — one filed to a house, one still sitting on the unfiled page under a house name. Removes the houseless copy. People genuinely waiting to be placed are left alone.

The Gazette

Finds go to Gazette review as suggestions to confirm or dismiss, never straight onto a person.

Extract Gazette references

Turns London Gazette links written into biographies into proper citations with issue and page.

Search The Gazette for honours

Searches only people whose entry already suggests an honour or public office — a national search returns a namesake for most residents. Hits are filtered to their lifetime. Takes a few minutes; running it again picks up where it stopped.
