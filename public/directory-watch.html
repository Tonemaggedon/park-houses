Architects & lace makers

The Park's own directory pages only see people once they live here. This follows each architect and lace maker the record knows — and a few prominent lace makers it does not yet — through their own entries in every Wright's Directory of Nottingham from 1858 to 1915-16, wherever they lived, so the year an address first falls in The Park stands out.

Read from the page text rather than the scans: a line can carry misreadings and a namesake can slip in, so every line links to its page.
