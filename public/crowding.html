Crowded houses

Properties holding an improbable number of people in a single census year. Usually this is several households filed against one address rather than a genuinely enormous one — the Pembertons at 1 Kenilworth Road were found this way.

Surnames is the stronger signal. A real household is rarely more than two or three families. Twenty people under twenty surnames is not a household.
