History of the estate

How the ground came to be built on: a castle rock the Saxons ignored, a ducal palace burnt by rioters, and then four hundred houses put up for the wealthy of a city that had just been made one.

This is the background to every house in the record. Where the map's column wrapped and the rest of a sentence was lost, the entry was left short rather than completed from guesswork; all of those have since been read off the original and finished. Any that ends in a red ellipsis is still waiting on one.
