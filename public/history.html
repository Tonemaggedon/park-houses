History of the estate

How the ground came to be built on: a castle rock the Saxons ignored, a ducal palace burnt by rioters, and then four hundred houses put up for the wealthy of a city that had just been made one.

This is the background to every house in the record. Entries ending in a red ellipsis are unfinished — the map's column wrapped and the rest of the sentence was lost, and they are left short rather than completed from guesswork.
