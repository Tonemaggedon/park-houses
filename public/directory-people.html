Directories by surname

Each surname in The Park, with everyone of that name in the census and every line Wright's Directory prints for it — anywhere in The Park, not just at one house. Each person's census and directory sightings are laid end to end, so a move, a misfiling or a missing household shows up across streets.

Forenames must agree, a middle initial on both sides must agree, and servants, visitors and boarders are never matched to a directory householder. Same person keeps a match; Not them passes it. Nothing else in the record changes.
