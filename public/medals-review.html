Medal cards

First World War medal index cards held at The National Archives in series WO 372, matched against the people this record already holds. A card gives a surname, forenames, a rank and a corps — and no birth year at all, so it can suggest a person but never identify one.

Nothing is written to anybody here. Confirming records the card against the person; passing it says this is not them, and neither is offered again.

Its own series filter is ignored by the service, so every result's reference is checked to begin WO 372/ before it is offered — an unchecked search returns nurses' service files and Chancery suits alongside the cards. A surname returning more than two thousand cards is skipped rather than guessed at: nothing on a card could narrow it.
