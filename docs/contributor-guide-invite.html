A guide for contributors

The record of The Park's four hundred houses and the people who lived in them is open to anybody who wants to work on it, and there is now a guide to what every page is for and what you can do on it.

Read the guide — it covers the working pages, the walk that takes the record out of doors, the discussion board, and where to start if you have an hour.

Three things worth knowing before you begin:

  • If you have a question about a house, ask it. Open the house on the map and press Ask about this house. A guess typed into the record is indistinguishable from a fact a week later; a guess in a thread is a question somebody can answer.
  • Needs work is a list of jobs somebody could finish in an evening, each saying what is missing and what would finish it.
  • Walk it gives you the houses nearest to you and the questions that can only be settled standing in front of them. Fifteen minutes is enough to be useful.

You can read everything without an account. To change anything, join the record and I will set you up.

This guide lives on the site at nottinghamparkhouses.com/guide, where it is always the current one.
